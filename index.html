The challenge

Legal teams are drowning in documents

Manual processing creates critical bottlenecks, multiplies the risk of human error and slows every decision that matters.

01

Explosive data volume

A single criminal case can span 200 to 2,000 documents. Millions of legal files are generated worldwide every year — each one requiring analysis and classification.

02

Manual routine

Lawyers spend up to 40% of their working time on document handling. A full analysis of one complex case takes weeks — often months.

03

Errors and risk

The human factor causes missed evidence, contradictions in testimony and discrepancies in financial records — precisely the gaps that decide outcomes.

Why generic AI doesn't solve it

⊗

Superficial answers and hallucinations

General-purpose chatbots produce plausible but false results: 42–65% accuracy and hallucination rates up to 33%. Verifying their output takes longer than drafting the document from scratch.

❄

No systemic approach

Point tools process isolated queries — they never see the whole case and don't connect sources such as email, messengers and scans.

⚿

Confidentiality risks

Uploading sensitive case data to public AI models creates leakage risks; most tools offer no data isolation guarantees at all.

Security

Security by architecture, not by promise

Parselex is engineered for the confidentiality requirements of legal and banking practice: Zero-Trust access, on-device anonymization and deployment inside your own perimeter.

⛨

LexZTNA — Zero-Trust access

No public endpoints. Workspaces are reachable only through the LexZTNA secure client — encrypted mesh tunnels, device verification and least-privilege access.

⚿

Ethics Firewall — PII redaction

Names, identifiers and account numbers are replaced with deterministic tokens on your device before transmission — one value maps to one token, so the AI keeps entity coherence. Re-identification happens locally on return.

⚙

On-premise deployment

Open-weight models run inside your infrastructure: inference and semantic search never call external AI APIs, and documents never leave your perimeter.

✓

Compliance and audit

Per-user data isolation, a full action journal and permission checks on every operation — designed to slot into information-security policies. Data minimization aligned with GDPR, CCPA and ABA Model Rule 1.6.
